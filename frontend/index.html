How to Use SheetMerge with Your Google Sheet

Follow this end-to-end walkthrough to link accounts, import your Google Sheet, dispatch sequential personalized campaigns, and inspect bidirectional status updates.

1

Step 1: Sign In to Workspace

Sign in using fast 1-click Google OAuth or enter your email to receive a 6-digit confirmation code (OTP). This creates your secure app workspace.

2

Step 2: Link Google Sender Accounts

In Section 1 of the dashboard, link 1 or more Google accounts. Each account adds 50 verified sends/day to your aggregate pool (e.g. 2 accounts = 100/day).

3

Step 3: Setup Google Sheet Columns

Create a Google Sheet with these standard headers in row 1:

Email | First Name | Last Name | Company | Merge Status

Leave the Merge Status column blank or set to UNPROCESSED for new rows.

Download Sample Template
4

Step 4: Share & Connect Google Sheet

In your Google Sheet, click Share (top right) and set General access to 'Anyone with the link can view'. Paste the URL in Section 2 and click 'Connect Sheet'.

5

Step 5: Compose & Dispatch

Click 'Compose Template' to draft your subject and body using personalized merge tags like {{First Name}} and {{Company}}. Click 'Dispatch Campaign' to start sequential sending with humanized jitter.

6

Step 6: Sync Back to Google Sheets

As each email is dispatched, its status updates to EMAIL_SENT. Click 'Export Updated CSV' to download and re-import into your Google Sheet to view live status on both platforms!

Service Compliances & Deliverability Shield

✓ Google & Yahoo 2024–2026 Bulk Sender Compliance

Requires SPF, DKIM 2048-bit, and DMARC alignment. Enforces strict zero-spoofing TLS handshakes.

✓ RFC 8058 One-Click List-Unsubscribe

Automatic inclusion of List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...> and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click headers.

✓ 50-Send Daily Verified Quota Guard

Protects free Gmail accounts by limiting verified sends to 50/day. Pools multiple accounts together to expand volume safely.

✓ Automatic 5xx Hard Bounce Suppression

SMTP 550 permanent rejections are suppressed instantly to protect sender reputation and are never subtracted from your daily quota.

✓ Humanized Jitter Delay (2.0s–5.0s)

Applies randomized jitter intervals between sequential dispatches to avoid detection by automated firewall spam filters.

✓ Zero Synthetic Data Guarantee

No mock accounts or fake recipients are ever inserted into your database. The application operates strictly with real user inputs.
